COMPANY OF THE DAY - Moderna

Moderna (Nasdaq: MRNA), the US maker of mRNA medicines, closed Thursday at USD 202.63 (-17.6% from USD 245.81) and fell as much as 25% intraday when full results of its melanoma vaccine, developed with Merck, leaked online before release. With Merck's drug Keytruda, it cut the risk of recurrence or death by 42% in a 1,100-patient trial. Merck closed -1.8% at USD 147.24.
Next catalyst: Two-year melanoma data are due at the ESMO cancer congress in Madrid on Saturday 24.10.
